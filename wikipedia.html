Artificial intelligence (AI) is a field of computer science focused on creating machines and software that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. These tasks include learning, reasoning, problem-solving and understanding language.

Introduction

Artificial intelligence is used to develop systems that can analyse information, recognise patterns and make predictions. Some AI systems follow predefined rules, while others learn patterns from data.

Modern AI includes technologies such as machine learning, computer vision and natural language processing. These technologies are used in many digital services.

History

The idea of intelligent machines has existed for centuries. AI became a formal field of research during the 1950s, when scientists began studying whether computers could perform tasks associated with human thinking.

Advances in computing power, data availability and algorithms later helped AI research grow. In the 21st century, machine learning and deep learning became important areas of development.

Types of AI

Machine learning

Machine learning allows computer systems to learn patterns from data and use those patterns to make predictions or decisions.

Natural language processing

Natural language processing helps computers analyse, understand and generate human language. It is used in translation tools and chatbots.

Computer vision

Computer vision enables computers to interpret images and videos, such as identifying objects in a photograph.

Applications

AI is used in healthcare to support medical research, in education to provide learning tools, and in transportation to assist navigation. It is also used by search engines, recommendation systems and digital assistants.

Advantages

AI can process large amounts of data quickly, automate repetitive tasks and help people identify patterns that may be difficult to notice manually. It can also support scientific research and improve certain services.

Challenges

AI systems can produce inaccurate results and may reflect biases present in their training data. Other concerns include privacy, security, transparency and the responsible use of automated decisions.
